# Uppidi Upload

A simple, cross-platform file uploader that works on your phone, tablet, desktop, or web browser. Upload images, videos, and other files to your choice of hosting services.

## What You Can Do

Upload your files to multiple hosting services without switching between apps. Uppidi lets you pick which service to use each time you upload, or stick with your favorite one.

Supported hosting services include:

- Catbox
- Uguu.se
- Tmpfile.link
- FreeImage.host
- TempSH
- HttpBin.org (for testing)

Each file upload shows real-time progress, and you can cancel any upload at any time if you change your mind or need to save bandwidth.

## Supported Platforms

Uppidi runs on:

- Android phones and tablets
- iPhones and iPads
- Windows, macOS, and Linux desktops
- Web browsers

On mobile devices, you can upload directly from your photo gallery or choose any file from your device. On desktop and web, you can select files from your computer. You can also share files directly to Uppidi from other apps.

## Getting Started

1. Download and install Uppidi on your device.
2. Open Uppidi and select which hosting service you want to use.
3. Enter any required settings for that service (for example, an API key or server address).
4. Select a file to upload from your device.
5. Tap upload and wait for it to finish.
6. Copy the link from the app to share your file.

## Settings and Configuration

Uppidi stores your hosting service settings securely on your device. Your files are never stored or viewed by Uppidi. Files go directly from your device to the hosting service you choose.

Some hosting services require extra setup. Most work without any configuration.

## Web Browser Version

Uppidi works in web browsers, but some hosting services cannot be used directly from a browser due to technical limitations (CORS restrictions). For these services, you can enable an experimental proxy in settings to work around this limitation.

## Upload History

Uppidi keeps a record of your recent uploads, including the file name, hosting service used, and the link to your file. You can view this history anytime to copy links or check what you have uploaded.

## Language Support

Uppidi supports English, Italian, and Turkish. Additional languages can be added.

## Privacy

Uppidi does not store, view, or keep copies of files you upload. Your files go directly from your device to the hosting service you select. Uppidi only stores your preference for which service to use and any settings you enter (like API keys), all encrypted on your device.

## Troubleshooting

If an upload fails, check:

- Your internet connection is working.
- Your hosting service settings are correct.
- You have enough space on your device.
- The file is not corrupted.

If you still have issues, the app shows an error message with details about what went wrong.
